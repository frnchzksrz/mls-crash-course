Clinical Chemistry 1 & 2

CC · 2nd & 3rd Year

Get Access

Course Description

Covers the biochemical analysis of blood and body fluids, focusing on carbohydrate, lipid, and protein metabolism, enzymology, electrolytes, and acid-base balance. Clinical correlation with endocrine, hepatic, and renal diseases is emphasized.

Key Topics

  • Enzymes and isoenzymes (ALT, AST, ALP, CK, LDH)
  • Glucose metabolism and diabetes mellitus
  • Lipid profile and cardiovascular risk
  • Liver function tests and interpretation
  • Renal function tests (BUN, creatinine, eGFR)
  • Tumor markers and endocrine assays

What You'll Learn

  • Operate automated chemistry analyzers
  • Interpret metabolic panels and liver profiles
  • Troubleshoot common analytical errors and interferences
  • Correlate biochemical results with clinical conditions
  • Apply quality control procedures in clinical chemistry
