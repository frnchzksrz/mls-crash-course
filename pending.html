Community Rules & Guidelines

MLS Crash Course is a shared learning space. While your request is pending, please read through the rules below. These apply to every activity on the site — the Study Hub, the Community forum, and direct messages.

1. Respect the community

  • Treat every member with courtesy — no harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks.
  • Disagree with ideas, not with people. Debate is welcome; disrespect is not.
  • Keep criticism constructive, especially on shared notes, thoughts, and case discussions.

2. Academic honesty

  • Do not post or share quiz answers, exam leaks, or material that would compromise another student's learning.
  • Course content is for personal study only. Redistribution outside this platform is not allowed.
  • When discussing cases, present your own reasoning — not just the final answer.

3. Content standards

  • No spam, no advertising, no self-promotion without permission.
  • No offensive, obscene, or NSFW content.
  • Keep posts on-topic — irrelevant content will be removed.
  • Cite sources when sharing information from journals or textbooks.

4. Privacy & confidentiality

  • Never share real patient information, including names, photos, or identifiable details.
  • Do not share other users' private information without consent.
  • Report suspicious accounts or content to the administrator.

5. Study Hub etiquette

  • Quiz attempts are personal — do not complete them on behalf of others.
  • Flashcards are spaced-repetition based. Use them honestly to reflect your true recall.
  • Case studies are learning tools. Discuss them openly; do not spoil answers for others.

6. Account responsibility

  • Keep your login credentials private.
  • You're responsible for all activity that happens under your account.
  • Violations may result in temporary suspension or permanent removal from the platform.

By continuing to use MLS Crash Course, you agree to abide by these guidelines. Thank you for being a part of this learning community.
